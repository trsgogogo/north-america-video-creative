# Methodology and scope

This workflow adapts general short-video creative techniques into North American English production briefs. It combines 36 hook families with four script formats: story, relatable comedy, education and process demonstration.

The source training materials are not distributed with this repository. Examples in this package are creative illustrations, not verified studies, customer results or claims of platform effectiveness.

## Adaptation principles

- Preserve audience relevance, concrete scenes, visible processes, narrative progression and useful payoffs.
- Replace fabricated authority, testimonials, scarcity and performance numbers with real evidence or clearly labeled illustration.
- Use declarative headlines and natural English as configurable editorial defaults, not universal audience facts.
- Separate observed video elements from interpretations of why they may work.
- Match product and audience before transferring a reference structure; create original wording and examples.
- Use actual performance data to guide iteration and state uncertainty.

## Scope

This is a short-video analysis and creative workflow. It does not replace market-entry strategy, establish product-market fit, guarantee virality, render finished videos, or authorize publication.
