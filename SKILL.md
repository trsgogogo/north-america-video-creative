# Short-Form Content OS

Turn accessible evidence into original short-form creative and a production handoff. Match the requested scope: a hook request does not require a full research pipeline.

## Select the route

Read [the brief](templates/brief.md) and reuse supplied facts. Defaults target US/Canadian English; follow explicit locale and format choices. Ask only for material gaps.

- Full research-to-script: [A00 orchestration](prompts/A00-orchestrator.md), [role routing](references/workflow.md), and only the required [role prompts](prompts/README.md).
- Supplied video: [teardown](references/video-teardown.md), [A06](prompts/A06-transcript-and-video-teardown.md), then creative drafting.
- Supplied article: [A08](prompts/A08-article-to-voiceover-adaptation.md) and [frameworks](references/frameworks.md).
- Hooks or script: [hook catalog](references/hooks.md), [scripts](references/scripts.md), and [A09](prompts/A09-hooks-and-original-scripts.md).
- Storyboard: [A11](prompts/A11-storyboard-and-production-handoff.md) and [handoff templates](templates/handoff-guide.md).
- Revise creative: [iteration guide](references/creative-iteration.md) and [A13](prompts/A13-creative-testing-and-iteration.md). Use actual results if supplied; otherwise produce hypotheses and variants.

## Shared operating rules

Use a shared request queue/cache for the two collection providers; avoid duplicate paid collection. Read [integrations](integrations.md) before external research. Without configured tools or budget, finish independent writing and provide a collection plan. Supplied evidence can be used without external collection.

Preserve source URLs, timestamps, metric definitions and missing fields. Apply [data standards](references/data-standards.md) to outlier claims. One snapshot does not establish growth; creator location or English comments do not establish audience geography. Transcript-only access cannot establish camera work or visual pacing.

Before drafting, connect product features to practical benefits and proof. Use a reference-transfer map to adapt the function of a reference, not copy distinctive language. Do not invent customer results, inventory, savings, offers or personal experience.

Default creative package: ten distinct hooks, shortlist three, one main script, and two variants when iteration is included. Use 36 hook families, four formats, five body structures and ten article frameworks as separate creative choices, not a Cartesian product. Timing is estimated until read aloud.

Pass final scripts, storyboards and revised variants through [A12 QA](prompts/A12-evidence-and-delivery-qa.md). Report actual completion, limitations and assets still needed. A production plan is not a rendered video or an executed test. Publication, paid tests and messages require authorization for those actions.

Roles may be performed sequentially by one assistant. Do not automatically launch independent agents or recurring tasks.

## Deliverables

Return the requested subset of evidence tables, audience insights, hooks, original script, storyboard, proof/asset list, QA and test variants. Use [templates](templates/handoff-guide.md) for consistent handoffs. The [full English handbook](docs/handbook.md) contains every operating prompt.
