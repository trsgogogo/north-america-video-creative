# Worked example: opening-shift checklist

This is a fictional product brief and editorial demonstration. No scraped research, real customer results or measured creative performance is claimed. Run it with supplied facts and a zero research budget.

## Brief

- Mode: script_only.
- Buyer: US independent coffee shop owners.
- Product: a reusable opening-shift checklist that lets staff record and review completed tasks.
- Desired action: download the checklist.
- Channel: Instagram Reels; target: approximately 30 seconds.
- Assets: a printable checklist, a counter and a phone camera; availability must be confirmed by the actual user.
- Not established: time saved, revenue, reduced errors, testimonials or software integrations.

## Ten hooks

1. Your opening shift deserves more than “I think someone did it.”
2. Put the opening routine somewhere the whole team can see.
3. The checklist is ready before the first coffee order.
4. One counter. Three people. One visible opening routine.
5. Make “are we ready?” a checklist conversation.
6. Stop making the opening routine live in one person's head.
7. Before the doors open, put the work on paper.
8. The smallest prop in this video is the one the team can share.
9. Coffee shops run on routines. This one fits on a page.
10. Give the next person a place to check what is done.

Shortlist: 1 for a recognizable scene, 2 for a clear product benefit, 10 for the handoff use case. These are editorial choices, not predicted performance rankings.

## Main script

> Your opening shift deserves more than “I think someone did it.”
> Put the routine on one checklist. Cups stocked. Counter ready. Opening tasks recorded where the next person can check them.
> This reusable template gives your team a shared place to record what is done before the doors open.
> Download the checklist and adapt it to your shop.

Format: education with a relatable opening. Duration is a planning estimate; time a real reading and revise before shooting. The example promises a shared record, not measured savings.

First frame: the checklist goes onto the counter next to the opening supplies.
